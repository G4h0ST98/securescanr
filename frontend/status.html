04 / Scope

What this page can and cannot see

A status page that overstates itself is worse than none.

Where the checks run

Every request on this page is made by your browser, from your network — not by a monitoring service somewhere else. A red row can mean our service is down, or that something between you and it is. Refresh from another network before assuming the worst.

No history is stored

The bar strips beside each service are only the checks this tab has run since you opened it. Nothing is recorded and there is no uptime percentage here, because we do not keep one. Leave the tab open and the strip fills in.

Third parties are opaque

Browsers will not let this page read a response from Razorpay, LemonSqueezy or Resend. We can only see whether the connection was refused. Those three therefore show reachability, sometimes without a latency figure, and never change the verdict at the top.

The scan row is a proxy

The Free Scan row checks /health rather than firing a real scan every minute — a live scan would take seconds and burn quota on every refresh. Degraded means slower than that service's own threshold, not an error.
